Proxo Downloader

Privacy Policy
01

General Overview

Proxo Downloader is developed and maintained as a Free, ad-supported service. This core utility is provided at zero cost to users and is completely optimized for fluid and lightweight media downloading functionalities.

02

Personal Data Collection

We operate on a strict privacy-first framework. The application runs entirely without any server-side authentication, mandatory profile sign-ups, or login procedures (Auth-free). No personal identification, email indexes, or phone registries are collected.

03

Anonymous Usage Model

All network URL parsing operations are executed directly on the client side or through isolated secure runtime sessions. Your input parameters and automated clipboard links are never tracked, recorded, or broadcasted to any external networks.

04

Third-Party Native Advertising

To support ongoing maintenance costs, we deploy Google AdMob SDK components. These safe systems may automatically review non-personal telemetry data, primarily device advertising identifiers (Advertising ID), to securely serve customized advertisements, avoid bot spam, and protect traffic integrity.

05

Data Retention Boundaries

Because there are no centralized databases or user accounts managed under this structure, persistent data retention is nonexistent. Any local download caches or temp file blocks exist purely within your application's designated sandbox storage directory.
